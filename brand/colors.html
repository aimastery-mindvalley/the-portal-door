Brand Principles

Quick reminders for anyone touching the visual system.

Royal blue is the room.

Never break out of the cosmic field. New sections layer on top of it with cards, never with solid panels.

Gold is sacred.

Reserve gold for titles, CTAs and the moments meant to be remembered. If everything is gold, nothing is.

Cyan whispers.

Cyan is for eyebrows, hairlines and one or two italic words — never headlines, never paragraphs.

Edges stay soft.

Borders sit at 12–30% opacity. Glows are blurred. Nothing on the page should feel hard-edged or stamped down.

Type breathes.

Serif for the story, sans for the signs. Uppercase labels always carry wide tracking (0.18em or more).

Motion is gentle.

Reveals fade up by 12–24px. Buttons lift 2px on hover. Nothing zooms, bounces or spins.
